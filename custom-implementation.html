Data retention

Keep signing data for as long as your policy says.

Your signed files, tracking list and audit archive stay in your SharePoint, under your own Microsoft 365 retention rules. These settings control the temporary copies on your bridge.

Security and control

Your IT team owns every layer.

Single organisation

The server, disk, database, secrets and backups serve your organisation only.

Your keys

The AES-256-GCM key is generated in your subscription and kept in your Key Vault. Once our setup access is removed, we can’t read your signing data.

Hardened runtime

Locked-down containers running without root, HTTPS only, and a hardened Linux server with a firewall and key-only administrator access.

Your monitoring

Send health checks and logs to your own Azure monitoring, such as Log Analytics or Microsoft Sentinel.

SharePoint stays delegated

Signed copies are still saved by your web part as the signed-in user. The bridge needs no SharePoint permissions and stores no SharePoint credentials.

Know the limit

The running server uses the encryption key, so anyone with full control of that server could read its data. A lost key cannot be recovered, so keep a secured emergency copy.

How we deliver it

Six phases, from scoping to handover.

We work through the rollout with your Azure and Microsoft 365 administrators.

1

Scoping

Agree the Azure region, setup, web addresses, mail service, retention periods, Microsoft sign-in arrangement and whether Claude is allowed.

2

Provisioning

With your Azure administrator, using limited access to one resource group: compute, Key Vault, logging and the backup target.

3

Hardening and keys

Deploy the containers, harden the server, set up HTTPS certificates and generate your encryption key.

4

Microsoft 365 setup

Add the SharePoint package, approve permissions, point the web part at your addresses and pin your hub site and Teams tab.

5

Acceptance testing

Run health checks, test email delivery, complete a live signing back to SharePoint, and restore a backup.

6

Handover

Provide as-built documentation, key rotation and recovery procedures, then remove our temporary access.

Setup options

Start simple, add what you need.

Hardened single virtual machine

The standard setup: one Ubuntu server in Australia East running the signing bridge, portal and web proxy, with Azure Key Vault and encrypted backups to Australia Southeast.

Azure Container Apps

For teams that prefer managed Azure services. Starts from our Bicep template and is designed, completed and tested with you as part of scoping.

Scoped add-ons

Azure Front Door with web application firewall (a global Microsoft service, so confirm it suits your residency rules), your branding on the portal and emails, and CRM or practice system integrations.

Know the boundary

What still leaves your Azure environment.

Your own bridge keeps the signing engine, database and backups in Australia. It does not cut you off from the internet. Include these in your privacy assessment.

Email to signers

Codes, reminders and completion emails, including signed PDFs up to 10 MB, go through your mail service to each recipient’s email provider, wherever it is.

Signers’ devices

Clients sign from their own browser, wherever they are, and can download their own copy.

Microsoft services

Azure and Microsoft 365 run under your Microsoft agreement and its data location commitments. Check where your SharePoint data is held.

Web certificates

The certificate authority receives your public web address, not your documents or recipient details.

Transform with Claude, if allowed

The PDF you select and your instructions go to Anthropic in the United States. Core signing never uses AI, and you can switch this off. Details.

Questions

Common questions.

Does signing change for staff or clients?

No. Staff send and track from the same SharePoint web part and Teams tab. Clients verify with an emailed 6-digit code and need no Microsoft login.

Can we start on the standard service and move later?

Yes. We deploy your bridge and you update the two web addresses in the web part settings. Finish or cancel envelopes in progress first. Your files in SharePoint stay where they are.

How is it priced?

A fixed implementation fee scoped to your requirements, a Send & Sign Custom licence for your organisation, and an optional managed updates and support agreement. Microsoft bills you directly for Azure.

Who runs it after handover?

Your team, or us under a managed updates agreement using only the access you grant.

Plan your Australian deployment.

Tell us your region, web address, mail service and retention requirements, and we’ll scope the work. You can try signing on the standard hosted service in the meantime.
