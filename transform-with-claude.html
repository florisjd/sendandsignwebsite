Voluntary add-on · Tier 3

Transform with Claude.

Turn a static PDF proposal into an interactive, mobile-friendly document before you send it. It only runs if you agree to it and someone on your team chooses to use it.

What gets sent See Tier 3 pricing

Your choice

You have to agree to it first.

Transform with Claude is a voluntary add-on in the Tier 3 subscription. You must agree to it before you use it, because using it sends your chosen document to an AI provider in the United States.

If you don't agree, or simply don't use it, all your PDF and HTML signing works as normal with no AI involved.

  • Tier 3: up to 100 transforms a month, with up to 5 refinements for each transform
  • Custom plans: allowance set in your agreement
  • 14-day trial: up to 10 transforms
  • Tier 1 and Tier 2: not included
  • No background, automatic or bulk scanning of your SharePoint libraries

How it works

You stay in control at every step.

1. Choose a document

Pick a PDF in SharePoint, choose a layout and add your instructions. A notice in the app tells you what will be sent before you click Transform with Claude.

2. Check and refine

Preview it on desktop, tablet and mobile. A text check compares numbers, amounts and wording with your original PDF and flags any differences.

3. Approve, then send

If differences are flagged, you must review and acknowledge them before you can approve. Nothing reaches your client without a person approving it.

Your information

What is sent to Claude, and what never is.

Information is sent to Anthropic only when a user clicks Transform with Claude or asks for a change to the result. It travels over an encrypted HTTPS connection from our signing bridge.

Sent when you use it

  • The whole PDF you select, including its text, pricing, logos and images, and any personal information inside it (or up to 32,000 characters of source text if no PDF is used)
  • The file name and the layout you chose
  • Your organisation name, if you have set one
  • The instructions you type
  • For changes: the current draft and your edit request

Never sent

  • Your Microsoft 365 sign-in tokens, user names, user email addresses or tenant ID
  • SharePoint site addresses, folder paths, tracking lists or any other files
  • Signing links, one-time codes, signer IP addresses, signature images or audit trails
  • Recipient names and email addresses, unless they are written inside the document you select
  • Licence, activation and billing details

AI sub-processor

Where it goes and how long it is kept.

ProviderAnthropic, PBC, through its commercial Claude API (model claude-sonnet-4-6)
LocationUnited States
Model trainingUnder Anthropic's commercial API terms, your inputs and outputs are not used to train its models
Retention by AnthropicUp to 30 days for trust and safety monitoring, plus a short-lived cache of about 5 minutes while your document is being generated
Retention by usDocuments, instructions and results are not written to disk. The preview is held in memory for up to 30 minutes on our signing bridge, which is not in Australia
What we recordMonthly counts of transforms and refinements, to apply your plan's allowance. No file names, instructions or content

Safeguards

Checks before anything is signed.

Active content removed

Scripts, embedded frames, event handlers and remote images are stripped from every result before it can be previewed or signed.

No AI in signing

Once you approve a document, signing, email verification, AUD and GST totals, SHA-256 fingerprints and save-back to SharePoint never call an AI provider.

Careful error logs

If Anthropic returns an error, we log only the status code, error type and request ID, never the response text.

Australian privacy

Before you say yes.

Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

If a document contains personal information, using Transform with Claude discloses it to Anthropic in the United States. This is a cross-border disclosure under Australian Privacy Principle 8 of the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Make sure you have any consents you need before you submit a document.

Your responsibilities

  • Do not submit sensitive information, such as health information, Tax File Numbers, other government identifiers or payment card numbers, unless you have all required consents and have decided that processing in the United States meets your obligations
  • Review every clause, figure, GST amount, date and party name before approving. AI output can contain errors
  • Get your own legal advice: neither Send & Sign nor Claude provides it
