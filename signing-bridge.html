Data handling disclosure · November 2026

Inside the signing bridge.

The temporary, encrypted relay that lets your clients sign without a Microsoft 365 login. See what it receives, how long it keeps it and where.

The signing bridge is not hosted in Australia.

How long data is kept Where it is processed

Principles

A temporary relay, not an archive.

Your SharePoint is the record

Your permanent signed copies live in your own Microsoft 365 SharePoint. The bridge deletes its copies on the schedule below.

Encrypted at rest

Each envelope, including documents, names, email addresses, IP addresses and the audit trail, is stored as its own AES-256-GCM encrypted record.

No raw secrets stored

Signing links, download links, signer sessions and one-time codes are kept only as SHA-256 hashes.

Your tenant only

Requests from SharePoint need a signed-in Microsoft Entra ID user from an onboarded organisation. One organisation cannot read or change another’s envelopes.

Australian simple contracts

Only Australian governing law (Commonwealth, State or Territory) is accepted for simple contracts under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth) s 10. Other codes are refused.

Fails closed

If sealing a document fails, nothing is marked signed. The failure is logged and the signer can safely try again.

What happens

From sending to deletion.

1. Check

The sender, organisation and plan are verified. The document’s fingerprint is recalculated and must match. HTML proposals are stripped of scripts and active content, and prices and required fields are locked.

2. Encrypt and store

The envelope is encrypted. Only lookup details (envelope and tenant IDs, status and dates) and hashes sit outside the encrypted record.

3. Verify the signer

The signer opens their link, receives a code by email and enters it before seeing the document.

4. Seal

Signatures are applied and a certificate and audit trail are added. Proposal totals and 10% GST are recalculated by the server. A new SHA-256 fingerprint is made.

5. Record and email

Each event is added to a hash-chained audit trail. The bridge emails codes, automated reminders (after 5 days), turn invitations and completion notices through its own mail service. The sender’s first invitations and manual reminders go from their Microsoft 365 mailbox. Signed PDFs up to 10 MB are attached; HTML proposals are sent as a link.

6. Return and delete

Your SharePoint web part saves the signed copy to the original folder using the sender’s own permissions. Once the fingerprint is confirmed, the original document is deleted from the bridge.

Protection

How it is protected, and the limits.

In transit

Connections to bridge.sendandsign.online and sign.sendandsign.online use TLS 1.3. Email is sent to the mail server over TLS.

At rest

AES-256-GCM encryption for every envelope and every backup of the database.

What encryption covers

The encryption key is held on the same server. It protects backups, disk copies and theft of stored data. It does not protect against someone gaining full control of the running server.

Australian law

What this means for your obligations.

Overseas disclosure (APP 8)

Personal information in an envelope is processed outside Australia. Take this into account in your privacy notice and any consents you rely on. Signers see an APP 5 privacy notice before they verify or sign.

Security and deletion (APP 11)

The deletion schedule, encryption and hashing described here are the steps used to limit what is held and for how long.

Records (ETA s 12)

Keep your long-term records in SharePoint. The bridge keeps envelope details for 90 days only, so it is not your retention system.
