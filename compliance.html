Evidentiary foundation

Four pillars, built around the ETA.

Every envelope records who signed, what they intended, what they consented to and what the document looked like.

I

Identity & attribution

Each signer receives a single-use 256-bit link tied to their email, then proves control of that inbox with a 6-digit one-time code (5 wrong attempts locks it) — supporting identification under ETA s 10(1)(a).

I

Intention & delivery

Consent and signing stay locked until the document opens on the signer's device. The signer confirms with a deliberate Sign and complete action.

R

Reliability & retention

Server-verified SHA-256 fingerprints, an RFC 8785 hash-chained audit trail, a multi-page certificate (UTC + AWST), and a signed PDF emailed to every party.

C

Consent & disclosure

Server-owned, versioned consent notices with a paper-copy option. Exact wording hash is recorded per signer.

Australian case law, designed in

The right person, provably.

In Williams Group Australia Pty Ltd v Crocker [2016] NSWCA 265, the court would not bind a person to an electronic signature that could not be shown to be theirs.

Send & Sign tethers every signing session to the specific recipient it was issued to with a mandatory 6-digit email code, real client IP and browser capture. The signer's identity on the certificate is always the token-bound, OTP-verified recipient — if someone types a different name, it is recorded separately as an "adopted" signature name.

Clear scope

Built for everyday agreements.

We'd rather be clear about what Send & Sign is for than leave you guessing.

Designed for

  • Commercial contracts and service agreements
  • Quotes and interactive HTML proposals
  • Non-disclosure agreements
  • HR agreements and offer letters
  • Internal and external multi-party sign-offs and approvals

Supported file types: PDFs and interactive HTML proposals only. Word, Excel and PowerPoint files must be saved as PDF first.

Not intended for

  • Deeds (including Corporations Act s 127), witnessed instruments, wills, powers of attorney, statutory declarations, affidavits and land-registry instruments
  • Some documents have further State and Territory rules: get legal advice if you are unsure

Australian privacy notice

Signers see an APP 5 collection notice. All invitation, code and receipt emails are strictly transactional.

Exportable evidence pack

The complete, hash-chained event history can be exported as a JSON evidence pack and verified independently, for audits or disputes.

Hosting & sub-processors

Our servers are not currently located in Australia. Ask us for the current hosting provider, location and sub-processors during evaluation.
