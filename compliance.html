Australian compliance

Engineered around the Electronic Transactions Act.

Built for simple contracts under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth) s 10 — every signing step maps to one of its four requirements.

Talk to us about your use case What you can send

ETA 1999 (Cth) s 10

Four pillars, four controls.

I

Identity

Each signer receives a single-use 256-bit link tied to their email, then proves control of that inbox with a 6-digit one-time code (5 wrong attempts locks it).

I

Intention

Signing is an explicit act: the signer places, adopts and confirms their signature with a clear Sign and complete action, recorded as its own audit event.

R

Reliability

SHA-256 fingerprints of the original and signed PDF, an RFC 8785 hash-chained audit trail, and an A4 Certificate of Electronic Signature on every document.

C

Consent

Before signing, each signer accepts a versioned electronic-records consent notice — with a clear option to sign on paper instead. Their acceptance is recorded.

Case law, designed in

The right person, provably.

In Williams Group Australia Pty Ltd v Crocker [2016] NSWCA 265, a signature applied by someone else through a shared system was not binding on the named person.

Send & Sign tethers every signing session to the specific recipient it was issued to. The signer's identity on the certificate and in SharePoint is always the token-bound, OTP-verified recipient — if someone types a different name, it's recorded only as an "adopted" signature name alongside the verified one.

Clear scope

Built for everyday agreements.

We'd rather be clear about what Send & Sign is for than leave you guessing.

Designed for

  • Commercial contracts and service agreements
  • Quotes and proposals
  • Non-disclosure agreements
  • HR agreements and offer letters
  • Internal and external multi-party sign-offs and approvals

Not intended for

  • Deeds, including company deeds under Corporations Act s 127
  • Documents that must be witnessed
  • Wills, enduring powers of attorney and advance health directives
  • Statutory declarations, affidavits and court documents
  • Land-registry instruments (use PEXA or another ELNO)

Privacy notice at the point of collection

Signers see a collection notice in the portal before any details are used, and invitation, code and receipt emails are strictly transactional — no marketing.

Exportable evidence pack

The complete, hash-chained event history can be exported as a JSON evidence pack and verified independently, for audits or disputes.

Hosting & data residency

Ask us for the current hosting region during evaluation. A dedicated deployment into your own Azure subscription — for example Australia East — is available.

Enterprise roadmap

Stronger assurance, on the way.

Today's evidence is the SHA-256 fingerprints, certificate page and hash-chained audit trail. These upgrades are planned for the Enterprise edition — they are not yet available.

  • PAdES B-LT digital sealing with a hardware security module (Azure Key Vault Managed HSM)
  • RFC 3161 trusted timestamps
  • SMS one-time codes via an Australian mobile number
  • Digital ID verification (myID / AGDIS)
