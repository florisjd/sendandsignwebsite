Your team browses existing SharePoint libraries, launches the 2-step New Envelope wizard, tracks live Viewed by signer updates, and inspects audit trails inside SharePoint, Microsoft Teams, or the SharePoint Mobile App.

Built for least-privilege IT approval

IT admins can rename the .sppkg to .zip and inspect every file. There is no server-side code in your tenant, no PowerShell script, and no blanket Sites.ReadWrite.All application permission handed to a third-party cloud.

Built around section 10 of the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth): explicit electronic-signature consent (or paper request), 6-digit email OTP verification, SHA-256 fingerprints, and interactive proposals with server-verified 10% GST.

Head-to-head

How Send & Sign compares against each alternative.

Send & Sign vs. Microsoft 365 eSignature

Microsoft's built-in eSignature keeps files inside SharePoint, but requires linking an Azure Pay-As-You-Go subscription and bills per signature request. Send & Sign gives you a dedicated live KPI dashboard in SharePoint and Teams, full mobile sending via the SharePoint Mobile App, up to 20 signers, and interactive AUD/GST proposals — with zero Azure billing setup.

Send & Sign vs. FuseSign

FuseSign is a strong Australian tool for accounting firms, and its SharePoint connector files signed documents back automatically. However, staff still leave SharePoint to build bundles in FuseSign's external app, and connecting it requires granting Sites.ReadWrite.All Application permissions across all site collections. Send & Sign keeps your team inside SharePoint and Teams with strictly delegated permissions.

Send & Sign vs. Annature

Annature offers local Australian support, but has no native SharePoint web part. Syncing SharePoint with Annature requires generating API keys, licensing Power Automate, and building and maintaining two separate cloud flows to send drafts and save completed PDFs back. Send & Sign works out of the box in 3–5 minutes with zero flows to maintain.

Send & Sign vs. DocuSign & Adobe Sign

Enterprise SaaS platforms bring per-user seat fees, envelope caps, and permanent storage of your client contracts in external clouds. With Send & Sign, a document passes through our signing bridge only while it is out for signature — encrypted with AES-256-GCM and deleted on a fixed schedule once the signed copy is saved back. Your team never leaves SharePoint, and the permanent record is filed in the folder the document came from.

When another tool might be a better fit

Send & Sign is purposefully built for simple contracts and everyday commercial or HR agreements under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth) s 10 (quotes, proposals, NDAs, service agreements, employment contracts). If your organisation specifically needs remote video witnessing for individual deeds, wills, statutory declarations, or government-grade biometric ID checks, a specialist legal signing platform is the right tool for those documents.
